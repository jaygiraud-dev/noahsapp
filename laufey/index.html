The Artist

Icelandic-Chinese, conservatory-trained, and quietly rewriting what a pop star can be.

Laufey Lín Bing Jónsdóttir grew up between Reykjavík, Washington D.C., and her grandparents' home in China — a cellist and pianist raised on Ella Fitzgerald and Chopin in equal measure. Out of that came a sound all her own: the warmth of mid-century jazz, the poise of classical music, and the intimacy of a bedroom pop confessional.

Since her 2021 debut EP Typical of Me, she has become a generational voice — landing back-to-back GRAMMY wins, selling out concert halls and now arenas across the globe, and proving that gentleness and virtuosity can top the charts.
