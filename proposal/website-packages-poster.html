A Local Auto Shop Website — Build Menu

From a Business Card
to a Revenue Engine

The feature ladder for a modern mechanic's website — start simple, then bolt on booking, loyalty and payments as the shop grows.

1

Starter Presence

The one-pager
A fast, mobile-first page that turns a Google search into a phone call.
  • Click-to-call & click-to-text buttons
  • Hours, address & embedded map
  • Services list with descriptions
  • Real shop & team photos
  • Simple contact form
  • Google reviews + social links
  • SSL, fast load, basic local SEO
  • Mobile-responsive design
2

Lead Generation & Trust

Upsell #1
Capture more enquiries and build instant credibility.
  • "Request a Quote" form (year/make/model)
  • Easy multi-step intake form
  • Photo upload of the problem / dash light
  • Live chat or chatbot
  • Live Google / Yelp review feed
  • Before / after gallery
  • Coupons & specials page
  • Warranty, certifications & FAQ
  • Maintenance blog (SEO)
  • Email / SMS list sign-up
3

Online Booking & Scheduling

The big value jump
Let customers book themselves in — 24/7 — without tying up the phone.
  • Real-time availability calendar
  • Service type sets the time block
  • Bay / technician capacity rules
  • Drop-off, wait, loaner & shuttle options
  • Auto SMS / email reminders (cut no-shows)
  • One-tap reschedule & cancel
  • Shop calendar sync (Google / Outlook)
  • Holiday & lunch blackout dates
  • Recurring service reminders
  • Deposit-to-book for big jobs
4

Customer Accounts & Loyalty

Retention engine
Turn one-time fixes into customers for life.
  • Customer portal with full service history
  • Multi-vehicle "garage" per account
  • Digital invoices, reports & photos
  • Loyalty / points / rewards
  • Referral program with codes
  • Maintenance memberships (recurring revenue)
  • "Your car is due" auto reminders
  • Review-request automation
  • Win-back & birthday offers
5

Payments & E-Commerce

Full platform
The whole job — quote, approve, pay — handled online.
  • Digital estimates & e-sign approval
  • Digital vehicle inspections (photo/video to phone)
  • Online invoicing & secure payment
  • Pay-by-link / text-to-pay
  • Financing / buy-now-pay-later
  • Parts & accessories store
  • Gift cards & service vouchers
  • Sell service packages online
  • Tire / parts order-ahead
  • Quote → cart → booking flow

Premium Add-Ons

Stack onto any package — most also carry a recurring monthly fee.
  • Local SEO & Google Business optimization
  • Google Ads / Local Services Ads management
  • Analytics & call-tracking dashboard
  • Shop-software sync (Tekmetric, Shopmonkey…)
  • AI phone answering / after-hours booking
  • Reputation management dashboard
  • Fleet / commercial B2B portal
  • Towing / emergency request button
  • Multilingual & accessibility (ADA)
  • Hosting, backups & security retainer
