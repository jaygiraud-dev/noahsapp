Good To Know

Frequently Asked

Where are you located? +

We're based at Full Throttle Auto Spa in North Vancouver. Book a time, bring your vehicle in, and we'll take care of the rest. Call us if you need directions.

What do you need from me? +

Just a spot to park the vehicle and access to it. For interior vacuum on some packages we may ask you to provide a vacuum — we'll confirm when you book.

How long does a detail take? +

A basic exterior wash is quick — usually under an hour. Premium details and the truck "Everything Package" take longer since we go deep inside and out. We'll give you a time estimate when you book.

How do I pay? +

Talk to us when you book and we'll sort out the easiest option for you. Cash and e-transfer are no problem.

Do you do trucks and big SUVs? +

Absolutely. Larger vehicles are priced under our "Everything Package" ($80–100) since there's more to clean. Call with your vehicle and we'll quote you.

How do I get the $5 off? +

Easy — post us to your Instagram (tag us in a story or post) and send us the screenshot when you book. We'll take $5 off your detail.
