Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your school schedule, the homework and plans you add, and, if you use them, friends and messages.
  • Parents never see friends, messages or chat photos. A parent only sees a student's agenda and homework after the student shares their code.
  • No ads. No selling or renting your information. No tracking or analytics tools.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.
  • You can ask us to see, fix or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

my agenda is a school agenda app made by Jay Giraud and Noah Giraud in North Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada ("we", "us"). We follow British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's privacy laws. Questions or requests: [contact email].

Who the app is for

my agenda is made for high school students and their parents or guardians. If you are under 13, please use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If we learn that a child under 13 is using the app without that permission, we will delete the account.

Who can see what

  • You see everything in your own account.
  • Friends you add see your first name, school, grade, points, streak and profile photo, plus messages and photos you send them. They don't see your homework or plans.
  • A parent you connect with (by giving them your code) sees your agenda: classes, homework (including details, due dates, whether it's done, and photos you attached), plans and events, points and streak, and the activity list. A parent can also send you nudges. A parent never sees your friends, your messages or chat photos. A connected parent can disconnect at any time.
  • Nobody else using the app can see your information. Everything is protected so that each person can only reach what they're allowed to.
  • We (the people who run the app) can access stored information when needed to run, fix or protect the app, or to answer your request. We don't read your messages unless you ask us to look into a problem or the law requires it.

Services we use

These companies process information for us, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and photo storage. Data is stored on servers in Oregon, USA.
  • Netlify: hosts the app's website.
  • Stripe: handles parent payments, including Apple Pay and cards. Card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications if you turn them on.
  • An email provider: sends sign-up and password-reset emails.

Because some of these services are in the United States, your information may be stored and processed there and may be accessible to authorities there under US law.

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we never use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete something in the app (homework, a plan, a message you unsend, a photo), it's removed from our database. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example, payment records Stripe keeps for tax purposes).

Keeping it safe

Information travels over encrypted connections. Our database uses access rules so each person can only reach their own information and what others have chosen to share with them. Photos are stored privately and shown through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and don't share your code with people you don't trust.

Your choices and rights

  • See or fix your information: most of it you can view and edit right in the app.
  • Delete things: delete homework, plans, photos and messages you sent at any time.
  • Disconnect: remove a friend at any time. A parent can disconnect from a student at any time.
  • Delete your account: email us at [contact email] from the email on your account.
  • Get a copy: email us and we'll send you a copy of your information.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings.

If you're not happy with how we handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
